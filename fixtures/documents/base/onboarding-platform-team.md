Welcome to the platform team. This guide covers your first two weeks.

Week one: get access to the repository and the knowledge base, read the architecture overview and
pair with your onboarding buddy on two small issues. Ask questions in the team channel; nobody
expects you to know the system yet.

Week two: take one shift as secondary on-call with your buddy as primary, review three pull
requests and present one thing you learned in the Friday demo.

Your manager schedules a check-in at the end of each week. Weekly status updates go on the team
wiki page, see the general FAQ.
