---
title: On-call handover guide - search
domains: ops, search
visibility: internal
---
At the end of a search on-call shift, write a handover note in the on-call log.

Cover open alerts, anything you silenced and why, index rebuilds in progress and planned changes
for the next shift. Keep it short: five bullet points are usually enough.

The incoming engineer acknowledges the handover by replying in the on-call log. If they have not
replied within an hour, call them.
