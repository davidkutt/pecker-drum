---
title: Incident response basics
domains: ops, process
visibility: internal
---
Declare an incident when customers are affected or when you are unsure. Declaring early is cheap.

Roles: an incident lead coordinates, a communications lead updates the public status page, and
responders work on the fix. One person may hold two roles in a small incident.

Status page updates go out at least every thirty minutes while an incident is open. Only the
communications lead marks an incident as resolved.
