Where do I post my weekly status update?
On the team wiki page at https://wiki.twin.example/status, by Friday noon.

How do I request access to a system?
Open an access request in the internal portal; your manager approves it.

Who do I ask about the release schedule?
The release manager of the month, listed on the release process page.

Can I work from another office?
Yes, tell your manager and book a desk in the office calendar.
