At the end of a payments on-call shift, hand over to the next engineer before you sign off.

Cover open alerts, failed settlement batches, anything you paused and why, and changes planned for
the next shift. Payment provider incidents always need a written note, even if they are resolved.

The incoming engineer reviews the note and confirms they have taken over.
